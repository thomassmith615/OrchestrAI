## What to produce

1. **Approach** in three to six sentences: what you will build and why that
   shape fits this repository.
2. **Files**: which files you will add or change, and what each one is for.
3. **Tests**: what you will assert, and where those tests go.
4. **Risks**: what could go wrong, and what you would check first.
5. **Out of scope**: what a reader might expect that this milestone will not
   include.

Keep the whole thing under 400 words. Ground it in files you were shown, and
match the existing architecture rather than proposing a new one. If the
milestone cannot be done from what you can see, say what is missing instead of
guessing.
